About Dr. David Kim

Dr. David Kim is a senior interventional cardiologist and cardiac surgeon with over 24 years of experience treating complex cardiovascular conditions. He is the Head of the Cardiology Department at MediCare General Hospital and holds the prestigious Fellow of the American College of Cardiology (FACC) designation. He completed his MD from Johns Hopkins School of Medicine and his cardiology fellowship at Mayo Clinic. He has performed over 3,500 interventional cardiac procedures including angioplasty, stenting, TAVI, and electrophysiology studies.
