About MediCare

A Legacy of Healing,
A Future of Innovation

Founded in 1984, MediCare General Hospital has grown from a 50-bed community clinic into one of the region's most trusted multi-specialty tertiary care hospitals. We serve over 300,000 patients annually across our main campus and three satellite clinics, bringing advanced diagnostics and world-class surgical expertise within reach of every family.

Our 500-bed state-of-the-art facility is equipped with the latest generation MRI, CT, PET-CT, robotic surgery suites, and a Level-I Trauma Centre. Our team of 150+ board-certified specialists collaborate through multidisciplinary tumor boards, cardiac teams, and neurological conferences to ensure every patient receives a holistic, evidence-based treatment plan.

Accredited by the Joint Commission International (JCI) and the National Accreditation Board for Hospitals (NABH), MediCare sets the gold standard for patient safety, clinical outcomes, and compassionate care throughout the region.

  • JCI & NABH Accredited – Meeting the Highest International Standards
  • Robotic Surgery, AI-assisted Diagnostics & Precision Medicine
  • 24/7 Level-I Trauma & Intensive Care Units
  • Integrated Electronic Health Records for Seamless Care Continuity
  • Dedicated Patient Relations & International Patients Desk
