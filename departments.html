About the Department

The Department of Cardiology at MediCare General Hospital is a nationally accredited centre of excellence for heart disease management. Our team of board-certified cardiologists and cardiac surgeons work collaboratively to provide the full continuum of cardiac care — from preventive screening and non-invasive diagnostics to complex interventional procedures and cardiac rehabilitation.

Specializations
  • Interventional Cardiology
  • Electrophysiology
  • Heart Failure Management
  • Cardiac Imaging & Echo
  • Preventive Cardiology
  • Cardiac Rehabilitation
