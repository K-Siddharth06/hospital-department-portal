Grievance Redressal Process

We take every concern seriously. Here's how to raise a grievance and what to expect.

  • 1
    Submit Your Grievance

    Fill out our online form, call our Patient Relations helpline at +1-555-100-1200, or visit Room 101 (Ground Floor, Main Building).

  • 2
    Acknowledgement

    You will receive an acknowledgement within 24 hours with a unique reference number for tracking.

  • 3
    Investigation

    A dedicated Patient Relations Officer will investigate and may contact you for additional details. This stage takes up to 7 working days.

  • 4
    Resolution & Feedback

    A written resolution will be shared with you. If unsatisfied, you may escalate to the Hospital Ombudsman at ombudsman@medicare-hospital.com.

Patient Rights

You have the right to receive complete and accurate information about your diagnosis, treatment options, and prognosis in a language you understand.

No medical procedure will be performed without your voluntary informed consent, except in life-threatening emergencies where you are unable to provide it.

Your medical records and personal information are protected. We comply with HIPAA regulations. Access to your records will only be provided to authorized individuals.

You have the right to refuse any medical treatment or procedure. You will be informed of the consequences of refusing treatment and asked to sign a release form.

You can book an appointment through our website by clicking "Book Appointment" in the navigation bar, calling +1-555-123-4567, or using our MediCare mobile app available on iOS and Android. Online booking is available 24/7 and confirmation is sent via email and SMS.

MediCare Hospital accepts over 150 insurance plans including Medicare, Medicaid, Blue Cross Blue Shield, Aetna, Cigna, United Healthcare, Humana, and most major commercial plans. Please contact our billing department at billing@medicare-hospital.com to verify your specific plan coverage.

General ward visiting hours are 10:00 AM – 12:00 PM and 4:00 PM – 7:00 PM daily. ICU visits are limited to 15 minutes per visit, twice daily, for immediate family only. Children under 12 must be accompanied by an adult. Special arrangements can be made for critically ill patients.

Medical records can be requested by visiting the Medical Records Department (Room 205, 2nd Floor) with valid photo ID, submitting a request via email to records@medicare-hospital.com, or through the patient portal at portal.medicare-hospital.com. Records are typically processed within 3–5 business days.

Appointments can be cancelled or rescheduled online through the patient portal, via phone at +1-555-123-4567, or by email. Please notify us at least 24 hours in advance to avoid a cancellation fee. Repeated no-shows may result in temporary suspension of online booking privileges.

Follow-up prescriptions are sent electronically to your preferred pharmacy.
